# Research Report

**Query:** Is chain-of-thought prompting an effective reasoning strategy for LLMs, or does it primarily improve output formatting? The literature disagrees—find the real fault lines and explain what accounts for the conflicting results.

## Summary

Research on chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting reveals conflicting evidence about whether it genuinely improves LLM reasoning capabilities or primarily improves output formatting. While some studies support CoT's effectiveness on reasoning-based tasks, other findings suggest its benefits are more nuanced than initially assumed, with criticisms noting that CoT may simply make implicit reasoning explicit without improving actual reasoning capacity. The literature disagreements likely stem from methodological differences across studies, though the evidence provided is limited in confidence, making it difficult to definitively determine the true nature and extent of CoT prompting's effects.
