# Research Report

**Query:** What are the real-world risks and benefits of using synthetic data to train or fine-tune large language models? Focus on data quality, bias, and evaluation.

## Summary

Our research findings suggest that synthetic data generation techniques, such as generative adversarial networks and masked language modeling, have achieved high perplexity, accuracy, and fluency scores in empirical studies evaluating large language models trained on synthetic data versus real-world data. However, the quality and fairness of synthetic data remain uncertain, and its impact on bias and fairness metrics in large language models across various real-world applications is not yet fully understood. While some studies indicate that synthetic data can be used to overcome data bottlenecks and address privacy concerns, there is also evidence of fidelity gaps and challenges in evaluating the performance of large language models trained or fine-tuned with synthetic data.
