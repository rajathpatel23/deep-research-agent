# Research Report

**Query:** What are the real-world risks and benefits of using synthetic data to train or fine-tune large language models? Focus on data quality, bias, and evaluation.

## Summary

The quality of synthetic data for training large language models is uncertain and varies depending on the generation technique used. While some studies suggest that Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) can capture complex data distributions and generate synthetic data that resembles real data, more research is needed to fully understand the potential risks and benefits of using synthetic data. The effectiveness of synthetic data in mitigating bias and improving performance in sensitive applications, such as healthcare or finance, also requires further investigation.
