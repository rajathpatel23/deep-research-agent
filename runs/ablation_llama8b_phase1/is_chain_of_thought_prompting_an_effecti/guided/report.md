# Research Report

**Query:** Is chain-of-thought prompting an effective reasoning strategy for LLMs, or does it primarily improve output formatting? The literature disagrees—find the real fault lines and explain what accounts for the conflicting results.

## Summary

Our research found that chain-of-thought prompting appears to be an effective reasoning strategy for large language models (LLMs) in certain contexts, particularly for tasks requiring multi-step inference. However, the existing literature also reveals limitations and inconsistencies in the findings, which may be attributed to differences in experimental design, task selection, and evaluation metrics. The effectiveness of chain-of-thought prompting in improving reasoning outcomes versus output formatting remains uncertain, with more research needed to fully understand its strengths and weaknesses.
